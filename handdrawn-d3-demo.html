click a legend dot to toggle a category · drag on the timeline below to zoom the years above

How this works: the lines and axes are driven entirely by D3 scales, exactly like a normal interactive chart — the brush below re-filters the domain, and the legend toggles series in/out, both of which recompute real scales. What makes it read as hand-inked is the render layer: each point gets a small per-point offset seeded from its own index (not re-randomized on every redraw, so it doesn't flicker while you interact), the path is drawn with a slightly wavering line generator instead of d3.curveLinear, and an SVG feTurbulence + feDisplacementMap filter roughs up the stroke edge. No rough.js — just a custom line generator and a filter, which keeps you in full control of exactly how "sketchy" it looks.
